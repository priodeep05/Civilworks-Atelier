Method notes & references

  • Pile capacity: fs = α·cu in clay with α = 0.5ψ−0.5 (ψ ≤ 1) or 0.5ψ−0.25 (ψ > 1), ψ = cu/σ'v, α ≤ 1; fs = β·σ'v in sand with a limit; qb = 9cu in clay, Nq·σ'v in sand with a limit. API RP 2GEO; Tomlinson & Woodward, Pile Design and Construction Practice.
  • Load test: Davisson, M.T. (1972) High capacity piles; ASTM D1143; AS 2159-2009 Section 8.
  • CPT: Robertson, P.K. (1990) Soil classification using the cone penetration test, Can. Geotech. J. 27(1); Robertson (2009) Can. Geotech. J. 46(11). Qt uses stress exponent n = 1.
