✦ Questions ✦

Is this live billing?

Not yet. This preview uses demo accounts stored in your browser and a free 14-day Pro trial. Subscriptions with card payments will be switched on when accounts move to a secure server.

Are the calculations certified?

No. They follow Australian Standards conventions for preliminary design, checking and teaching. A qualified engineer must review any design.

Can I cancel?

Yes — plans will be month-to-month or annual, cancel any time. Your downloads remain yours.
